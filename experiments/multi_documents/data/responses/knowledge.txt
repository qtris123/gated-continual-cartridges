1. Q: AMD's 4th Gen EPYC 9004 Series processors are built on the "Zen 4" core, and PepsiCo lists SodaStream among its key brands. In what year was the "Zen 4" microarchitecture first publicly launched by AMD, and in what country was SodaStream originally founded?
   A: Zen 4 launched in 2022, and SodaStream was founded in Israel (originally the United Kingdom in 1903, but the modern company is Israeli-based, headquartered in Israel).

2. Q: Both AMD (ticker: AMD) and PepsiCo (ticker: PEP) trade on the Nasdaq. In what year was the Nasdaq stock exchange founded?
   A: Nasdaq was founded in 1971.

3. Q: AMD completed its acquisition of Xilinx on February 14, 2022. In what year was Xilinx originally founded, and who were its co-founders?
   A: Xilinx was founded in 1984 by Ross Freeman and Bernie Vonderschmitt.

4. Q: AMD's CEO, Dr. Lisa Su, holds a doctoral degree, and PepsiCo's CEO, Ramon Laguarta, previously worked at Chupa Chups, S.A. From which university did Lisa Su earn her Ph.D., and in which country is Chupa Chups headquartered?
   A: Lisa Su earned her Ph.D. from MIT (Massachusetts Institute of Technology), and Chupa Chups is headquartered in Spain (Barcelona).

5. Q: AMD is headquartered in Santa Clara, California, and PepsiCo's principal executive office is in Purchase, New York. In which counties are these two cities located?
   A: Santa Clara is in Santa Clara County, California, and Purchase is in Westchester County, New York.

6. Q: PepsiCo reports a joint venture with Strauss Group for Sabra refrigerated dips and spreads. In which country is Strauss Group headquartered?
   A: Strauss Group is headquartered in Israel.

7. Q: AMD relies on Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited (TSMC) as its primary wafer foundry, and PepsiCo has a manufacturing plant in Lehavim, Israel. In what city is TSMC headquartered, and what is the approximate population of Lehavim?
   A: TSMC is headquartered in Hsinchu, Taiwan. Lehavim has a population of approximately 10,000-12,000.

8. Q: PepsiCo began distributing Hard MTN Dew in 2022, an alcoholic beverage manufactured by the Boston Beer Company. In what year was the Boston Beer Company founded, and who founded it?
   A: The Boston Beer Company was founded in 1984 by Jim Koch.

9. Q: AMD completed its acquisition of Pensando Systems on May 26, 2022. Who was the CEO/co-founder of Pensando Systems at the time of the acquisition?
   A: Prem Jain was the co-founder and CEO of Pensando Systems.

10. Q: PepsiCo's PBNA division manufactures and distributes brands licensed from Keurig Dr Pepper Inc., including Dr Pepper. In what year was Dr Pepper first created as a soft drink?
   A: Dr Pepper was first created in 1885.

11. Q: AMD was incorporated in Delaware on May 1, 1969, and PepsiCo was originally incorporated in Delaware in 1919. What is the capital city of Delaware, and when did Delaware become the first state to ratify the U.S. Constitution?
   A: The capital of Delaware is Dover, and Delaware ratified the Constitution on December 7, 1787.

12. Q: AMD's independent auditor is Ernst & Young LLP (serving since 1970) and PepsiCo's is KPMG LLP (serving since 1990). In what cities are the global headquarters of Ernst & Young and KPMG located?
   A: Ernst & Young (EY) is headquartered in London, United Kingdom, and KPMG is headquartered in Amstelveen, Netherlands.

13. Q: PepsiCo sold its Tropicana and Naked juice brands to PAI Partners in 2022. In what country is PAI Partners headquartered?
   A: PAI Partners is headquartered in France (Paris).

14. Q: AMD developed the semi-custom SoC products that power the Sony PlayStation 5 and Microsoft Xbox Series X/S consoles. In what year was Sony Interactive Entertainment's PlayStation brand first launched (original PlayStation console)?
   A: The original PlayStation was first launched in 1994 (December 3, 1994, in Japan).

15. Q: PepsiCo's CEO Ramon Laguarta has served as a director of Visa Inc. since 2019, and AMD also has a Wafer Supply Agreement with GLOBALFOUNDRIES. In what year was Visa Inc. founded, and in what year was GLOBALFOUNDRIES spun off from AMD?
   A: Visa was founded in 1958 (as BankAmericard), and GLOBALFOUNDRIES was spun off from AMD in 2009.

16. Q: AMD partnered with Valve to create a semi-custom APU for the Steam Deck handheld gaming device, and PepsiCo produces ready-to-drink coffee products through a joint venture with Starbucks. In what year was Valve Corporation founded, and in what city was the first Starbucks store opened?
   A: Valve was founded in 1996, and the first Starbucks opened in Seattle, Washington, in 1971.
