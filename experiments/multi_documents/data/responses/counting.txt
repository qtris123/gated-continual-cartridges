1. Q: How many times does the word "acquisition" appear in AMD's 10-K compared to PepsiCo's 10-K?
   A: "Acquisition" appears approximately 122 times in AMD's filing versus 62 times in PepsiCo's — roughly twice as often in AMD's, reflecting the dominance of the Xilinx and Pensando acquisitions in AMD's 2022 narrative.

2. Q: Which document has a higher total word count, and by approximately what ratio?
   A: PepsiCo's 10-K has approximately 184,838 words compared to AMD's approximately 62,420 words — PepsiCo's filing is roughly 3 times longer.

3. Q: How many distinct risk factors does each company enumerate in its Item 1A section?
   A: AMD's Risk Factors Summary lists 44 individual bullet-point risk items across six categories. PepsiCo lists approximately 25 individually titled risk factor sections under two categories (Business Risks and Financial Risks).

4. Q: How many times does the word "adversely" appear in each filing, and which company uses more cautionary language relative to its document length?
   A: "Adversely" appears 99 times in AMD's filing and 76 times in PepsiCo's. Relative to word count, AMD uses "adversely" once per approximately 630 words, while PepsiCo uses it once per approximately 2,430 words — making AMD's filing roughly 3.8 times more dense with this cautionary term.

5. Q: Compare how many times each company mentions its primary competitor by name. How many times does "Intel" appear in AMD's filing versus "Coca-Cola" in PepsiCo's?
   A: "Intel" appears 34 times in AMD's 10-K, while "Coca-Cola" appears only 3 times in PepsiCo's. AMD names its primary rival over 11 times more frequently than PepsiCo names its own.

6. Q: How many own-brand trademarks does each company explicitly list in its intellectual property section of Item 1?
   A: PepsiCo lists approximately 131 brand names in its trademark paragraph (from Agusha to Yachak). AMD lists approximately 34 product and technology trademarks (from AMD to Zynq). PepsiCo enumerates roughly 4 times as many brand names.

7. Q: Which term appears more often across both filings combined — "million" or "billion"?
   A: "Million" appears 281 times combined (160 in AMD, 121 in PepsiCo), while "billion" appears 232 times combined (111 in AMD, 121 in PepsiCo). "Million" is used approximately 21% more frequently overall.

   A: PepsiCo's filing contains approximately 1,005 dollar signs while AMD's contains approximately 602. However, per 1,000 words, AMD has roughly 9.6 dollar signs versus PepsiCo's 5.4 — making AMD's filing more financially dense per unit of text.

9. Q: How many times does the word "sustainability" or "sustainable" appear in each filing?
   A: Forms of "sustainab-" appear 29 times in PepsiCo's filing but only 3 times in AMD's — nearly 10 times more in PepsiCo's, reflecting its pep+ sustainability strategy as a central theme.

10. Q: How many pages is each company's 10-K filing, and what is the ratio of PepsiCo's page count to AMD's?
    A: AMD's 10-K is 121 pages and PepsiCo's is 503 pages. PepsiCo's filing is approximately 4.2 times longer by page count.

    A: AMD includes 17 notes (Note 1 through Note 17) while PepsiCo includes 14 notes (Note 1 through Note 14). AMD has 3 more notes, primarily because it has dedicated notes for Business Combinations, Concentrations of Credit Risk, and Contingencies that PepsiCo addresses within other notes.

13. Q: How many times does "Xilinx" appear in AMD's 10-K compared to how many times "Tropicana" appears in PepsiCo's 10-K?
    A: "Xilinx" appears 154 times in AMD's filing, while "Tropicana" appears only 13 times in PepsiCo's — a ratio of roughly 12 to 1, reflecting how the Xilinx acquisition permeated nearly every section of AMD's filing while PepsiCo's Tropicana divestiture was a smaller part of its overall narrative.

15. Q: How many times does the word "impairment" appear in each document?
    A: "Impairment" appears 139 times in PepsiCo's filing versus 55 times in AMD's — roughly 2.5 times more often in PepsiCo's, driven by its extensive SodaStream brand impairment, Russia-Ukraine conflict charges, and franchise rights impairment testing discussions.

16. Q: How many times does the word "dividend" appear in each filing?
    A: "Dividend" appears 17 times in PepsiCo's 10-K but only 3 times in AMD's. This reflects PepsiCo's long history of consecutive quarterly dividend payments since 1965 and its $6.2 billion in dividends paid in 2022, versus AMD which does not pay dividends and only mentions the term in passing contexts.
