1. Q: What were the total net revenues reported by AMD and PepsiCo for fiscal year 2022?
   A: AMD reported $23,601 million (approximately $23.6 billion) and PepsiCo reported $86,392 million (approximately $86.4 billion).

2. Q: Who served as the independent registered public accounting firm for AMD and PepsiCo, respectively?
   A: Ernst & Young LLP audited AMD and KPMG LLP audited PepsiCo.

3. Q: In which state is AMD incorporated and in which state is PepsiCo currently incorporated?
   A: AMD is incorporated in Delaware and PepsiCo is incorporated in North Carolina.

4. Q: What was the par value per share of common stock for AMD and PepsiCo, respectively?
   A: AMD's par value is $0.01 per share and PepsiCo's par value is 1⅔ cents per share.

5. Q: How many employees did AMD and PepsiCo each have worldwide as of December 31, 2022?
   A: AMD had approximately 25,000 employees and PepsiCo had approximately 315,000 employees.

6. Q: What were the total assets reported by AMD and PepsiCo on their respective balance sheets as of the end of fiscal year 2022?
   A: AMD reported $67,580 million and PepsiCo reported $92,187 million.

7. Q: What was the goodwill balance on the balance sheet for AMD and PepsiCo as of the end of fiscal year 2022 (in millions)?
   A: AMD reported $24,177 million and PepsiCo reported $18,202 million.

8. Q: What were AMD's and PepsiCo's gross profit for fiscal year 2022 (in millions)?
   A: AMD's gross profit was $10,603 million and PepsiCo's was $45,816 million.

9. Q: Who was the Chair/CEO of AMD and the Chairman/CEO of PepsiCo at the time of the 2022 10-K filings?
   A: Dr. Lisa T. Su for AMD and Ramon L. Laguarta for PepsiCo.

10. Q: What was the annual effective tax rate for AMD and PepsiCo for fiscal year 2022?
   A: AMD's effective tax rate was (10%) (a tax benefit) and PepsiCo's annual tax rate was 16.1%.

11. Q: What percentage of net revenue came from international/outside-U.S. operations for AMD and PepsiCo in fiscal year 2022?
   A: AMD's international sales were 66% of net revenue and PepsiCo's operations outside the U.S. generated 43% of net revenue.

12. Q: Since what year has each company's respective auditor served in that role — Ernst & Young LLP for AMD and KPMG LLP for PepsiCo?
   A: Ernst & Young LLP has served as AMD's auditor since 1970 and KPMG LLP has served as PepsiCo's auditor since 1990.

13. Q: What was the net income for AMD and the net income attributable to PepsiCo for fiscal year 2022 (in millions)?
   A: AMD's net income was $1,320 million and PepsiCo's net income attributable to PepsiCo was $8,910 million.

14. Q: How many reportable segments does AMD have versus PepsiCo?
   A: AMD has four reportable segments and PepsiCo has seven reportable segments.

15. Q: What was the total purchase consideration for AMD's acquisition of Xilinx and the upfront cash payment for PepsiCo's acquisition of Rockstar Energy Beverages?
   A: AMD's Xilinx acquisition had a total purchase consideration of $48.8 billion, and PepsiCo paid approximately $3.85 billion upfront for Rockstar.

16. Q: What was the diluted earnings per share for AMD and PepsiCo for fiscal year 2022?
   A: AMD's diluted EPS was $0.84 and PepsiCo's diluted EPS was $6.42.
